Optional Google Analytics 4

We load Google Analytics only if you explicitly select “Allow optional analytics” on this Privacy Notice page. If you consent, Google LLC may process website page views, browser/device information, approximate location based on network data, session cookies such as _ga and _ga_*, and non-identifying events such as calculator-card interest, contact-button interactions and general engagement. It does not erase data processed before withdrawal.
