Website Privacy Notice

Effective upon publication: October 29, 2026. Operated by Aule Engineering Consultants, LLC, Florida, USA. This notice applies to auleengineering.com and www.auleengineering.com.

Site operation and contact

Our website and hosting service process basic technical requests such as browser details, IP address and access times to serve pages and protect against abuse. If you call, email or submit project details separately, we use that information to respond and evaluate the requested engineering services. Do not submit client plans or confidential project information through website analytics.

Website usage analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) to understand aggregate traffic and visitor interest in our public engineering consulting website. Analytics is on by default unless you turn it off below or your browser blocks it. Google may process browser/device details, approximate network location, and pseudonymous first-party cookie identifiers (such as _ga or _ga_*).

The website records page views, whether visitors scroll to engineering service descriptions (Pool & Spa, Civil & Site, Construction Engineering, or Project & Owner Representation), visibility of the Copestone and StormRoute calculator cards, clicks to open those calculators, requests to view the contact section, phone/email copy-button clicks, navigation selections, and scroll-depth milestones at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 90%.

These interactions represent general interest and inquiry intent, not confirmed project inquiries, successful calculator launches, completed services, or accepted proposals. Calculator usage on separately hosted sites is not monitored here.

Event details use fixed labels for service, calculator, section and contact method (phone or email); page URLs omit query strings; referral information is reduced to the referring website origin; and calculator destinations are reduced to domain names. We do not send visitors' phone numbers, email addresses, names, project plans, engineering calculations, free-text project descriptions, uploaded documents, contact messages, or billing information in custom GA4 events.

A functional preference cookie (aule_analytics_preference) remembers whether you have switched analytics on or off across auleengineering.com and www.auleengineering.com; local browser storage also preserves a previous choice where available. We do not use Google Signals, advertising personalization, advertising cookie storage, or retargeting through this website tag. We do not sell website analytics. Google Analytics may still process personal data, including pseudonymous identifiers. See Google's Privacy Policy.
