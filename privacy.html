Basic website traffic analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) to measure aggregate traffic on this public engineering business website. The website loads analytics without displaying a popup or consent banner, unless you have previously disabled analytics using the controls below. Google may receive page visits, browser/device information, approximate location from network information, and a pseudonymous first-party cookie identifier such as _ga or _ga_*. The site sends only a basic page-view event without names, engineering project documents, phone numbers, email addresses, contact-form contents, or full URL query strings.

Google Analytics is used for general website-traffic totals, not advertising audiences, retargeting or user-level identification. Google Signals and advertising personalization are disabled in the tag. We do not associate website traffic with customer inquiries or billing. Google Analytics may still process personal data such as cookie identifiers; the service provider explains its practices in Google's Privacy Policy.
